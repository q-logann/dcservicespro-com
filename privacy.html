Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you send us through this website.

Who we are

This website is operated by DC Services Roof and Gutter Cleaning, LLC, a gutter cleaning and roof debris removal company serving metro Atlanta, Georgia. You can reach us at 1 (800) 518-6030.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our quote forms. On the residential quote form, that is your name, your address or ZIP code, and an email address or phone number. On the commercial estimate form, that is your business or property name, contact name, role, property type, approximate size, an email address or phone number, and any notes you choose to add.

Do not include payment details, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal information in a quote request.

How we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your quote or estimate request and to schedule and complete work you ask us to do. We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it with anyone for advertising.

Who else sees it

This website is hosted by Vercel. When you submit a form, the details are delivered to our business email inbox using Resend, an email delivery service. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process technical or form data only as needed to provide their services.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run analytics software that profiles you across other sites.

How long we keep it

Quote requests may stay in our email records so we can service and follow up on the work. You can ask us to delete your information, subject to legal and ordinary business-record requirements.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request deletion. Call 1 (800) 518-6030 or email derrick@dcservicespro.com.

Children

This site is intended for homeowners and property managers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page. This policy was last updated on July 19, 2026.
